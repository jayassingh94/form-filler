## Full description

Tired of typing the same details into every job, exam and admission form? Form Filler fills them for you, from a profile you save once, without your details ever leaving your computer.

**Fill web forms in one click**
• Names, dates of birth (in any format: DD/MM/YYYY, separate day/month/year boxes…), dropdowns, radio buttons, checkboxes and addresses
• Education (10th, 12th, graduation…), category, family and work details
• Fills the values and tells you in the popup which fields still need you (“Filled 12 fields · 2 need your input: Mobile, Course”). The form is never submitted for you
• Password, card, CVV, bank, OTP and captcha fields are never touched

**Fill PDF forms too**
• Fillable PDFs are filled directly
• Flat and scanned PDF forms: values are written on the printed lines and boxes
• Bilingual (English / Hindi) forms, and Hindi text in your profile
• Officer sections (“Certifying Officer”, “For office use only”) are left blank
• Check every field in a neat list, drag boxes to adjust, then download

**Photo & signature at the exact size**
• Pick documents from your encrypted Document Locker right next to any upload field
• Crop and resize to the form's pixels and KB range (presets for IBPS, SSC, UPSC, RRB, NEET), clean signatures to a white background, all on your computer

**Private by design**
• Your profiles and documents are stored only on your computer, encrypted with your PIN (AES-256), and lock automatically after 5 minutes
• AI only sees the form's own labels (and, if you agree, a blank PDF form). Never your details
• Use the free built-in AI (10 uses a day), your own Gemini API key, or switch AI off and use offline matching
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Several profiles (you and your family), encrypted backup and restore, “Delete all data” in one click

How it works: save your details once → open any application form → click Form Filler → “Fill this form” → fill in what the popup lists → submit yourself.
