Form Filler Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · applies to Form Filler 1.2 and its AI service

In short: your details, documents and PIN stay on your computer, encrypted. They are never sent anywhere. To match form fields, Form Filler may send the form's own text (field labels, option texts) and, only if you agree, images of a blank PDF form to an AI. By default that goes through Form Filler's small server, which passes it to Google Gemini and keeps nothing. You can use your own Gemini key instead, or switch AI off completely. There is no analytics, tracking, advertising or account.

1. What Form Filler stores, and where

  • Profiles: the personal, family, address, contact, education, category, work and custom details you enter.
  • Documents: files you add to the Document Locker (photo, signature, marksheets, certificates) and their names and tags.
  • Settings: your AI choice, and your Gemini API key if you use your own.
  • An anonymous install ID (a random number, see section 5) and today's count of free AI uses.

All of this stays in your Chrome profile on this computer (chrome.storage.local and the extension's IndexedDB). It is not synced to your Google account or any cloud, and Form Filler's developer never receives it.

2. How it is protected

  • Profiles, documents and settings are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your PIN (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 rounds, random salt). Your PIN is never stored.
  • When unlocked, the key is kept only in memory and is erased when you lock, after 5 minutes without use, or when you close Chrome.
  • After several wrong PINs you must wait before trying again.

Honest limits: a short, numbers-only PIN can be guessed by someone who copies your Chrome profile files. Use a longer PIN, ideally with letters. Encryption can't protect against malware already on your computer, or someone using it while Form Filler is unlocked. A forgotten PIN means the data can't be recovered.

3. What leaves your computer

Only when you use an AI feature, and only the form's own content: never your profile values, documents, PIN, the page address, or anything you typed into a form. Where it goes depends on Settings → AI matching:

Your values are filled in on your computer after the AI answers which field is which.

4. Form Filler's AI server

The free AI option uses a small server run by Form Filler's developer (hosted on Render). Its code is part of the extension's source, and it works like this:

  • It accepts only the three kinds of requests above (field labels, a blank page image, upload-rule text). It builds the AI prompt itself and ignores anything else in a request, so it can't be used as a general chatbot. As a safety net it masks anything that looks like an e-mail address, phone number or long number.
  • It passes the request to Google Gemini using the developer's API key and returns only Gemini's answer.
  • It stores nothing: no requests, answers, images or labels are saved or logged. Its log records only the time, the request type, the result code and how long it took. No install ID, IP address or content.
  • Daily limits are counted in the server's memory only, using a hashed install ID and your IP address hashed with a secret that changes every day and is never saved. These counters are deleted every day at midnight UTC and whenever the server restarts.
  • Hosting: like any website, the hosting provider (Render) handles the connection and may keep standard access records (IP address, time, request path) under its own privacy policy. These never include the request content.

5. The anonymous install ID

When you first use the free AI, Form Filler creates a random ID (for example 3f1c2a9e-…) and sends it with each free AI request, only so that the daily limit of 10 uses can be applied per installation. It is not linked to your name, e-mail, Google account or profile, is not used for anything else, and is deleted when you remove the extension or use Delete all data.

6. Google Gemini

Requests are processed by Google under the Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy.

  • Free AI option: Form Filler's server uses a Gemini API key with billing enabled (Google's “paid” tier). Under Google's terms, paid-tier prompts and responses are not used to improve Google's products.
  • Your own key: your own Google account's terms apply. On Google's free tier, Google may use submitted content (here only labels, option texts or blank-form images) to improve its products.

7. Fields Form Filler never fills, and what it never does

  • Never fills passwords, card numbers, expiry dates, CVV, bank account numbers, IFSC/SWIFT/IBAN, UPI/ATM PINs, OTPs, security questions or captchas.
  • Never fills officer or authority sections of PDF forms (e.g. “Certifying Officer”, “For office use only”).
  • Never submits a form. You always review and submit yourself.
  • Never runs on a website until you click Fill this form there. Chrome asks your permission per site, and you can remove it any time (Settings → Sites with access). Clicking the icon lets it see only the current tab's address, to ask for that permission.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, tracking or remote code. Nothing is sold or shared.

8. Fill a PDF and the Document Locker

  • A PDF you open is kept only in that tab's memory, unless you save the filled copy to your locker. It is cleared when Form Filler locks.
  • Values are written onto the PDF on your computer; the download goes straight to your computer.
  • “Pick from locker” opens in a protected extension frame the website can't read. A document is given to a page only when you click Attach. Cropping, resizing and compressing photos and signatures happen on your computer.

9. Backups, export and deletion

  • Export backup saves one file that stays encrypted with your PIN. Anyone with the file and that PIN can read it.
  • Import backup replaces everything with the backup's contents.
  • Delete all data erases profiles, documents, settings, your PIN data, the install ID and site permissions from this browser. Uninstalling the extension also removes its data. Form Filler's server holds nothing about you to delete.

10. Children

Form Filler doesn't collect data from anyone. Profiles you create for family members stay on your computer like everything else.
