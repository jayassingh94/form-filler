Form Filler Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: your details and documents stay on your computer, encrypted with your PIN. The only thing that ever leaves your computer is the text of a form's field labels and dropdown options, sent to Google Gemini when you click Fill this form (and only if you have added a Gemini API key). Your answers, files and PIN are never sent anywhere. There is no analytics, tracking, account, or server run by Form Filler.

1. What Form Filler stores

  • Profiles: the personal, family, address, contact, education, category, work and custom details you type in.
  • Documents: files you add to the Document Locker (photo, signature, marksheets, certificates) with their names and tags.
  • Settings: your Gemini API key and model name.
  • Site access list: managed by Chrome itself (sites where you allowed Form Filler to run).

2. Where and how it is stored

  • Only inside your Chrome profile on this computer: chrome.storage.local for profiles and settings, and the extension's IndexedDB for document files. Nothing is synced to your Google account or any cloud.
  • Everything is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your PIN (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 rounds, random salt). Your PIN itself is never stored.
  • When you unlock, the key is kept only in Chrome's in-memory session storage (never written to disk) and is erased when you click Lock, after 5 minutes without use, or when you close the browser.
  • After several wrong PINs, Form Filler makes you wait before trying again.

Honest limits: a short, numbers-only PIN can be guessed by someone who copies your Chrome profile files and tries PINs offline. Use a longer PIN, ideally with letters. Encryption cannot protect you from malware already running on your computer, or from anyone using your computer while Form Filler is unlocked. If you forget your PIN, your data cannot be recovered.

3. What is sent to Google Gemini (and what is not)

Sent (only when you click Fill, only with an API key)Never sent
  • Each form field's label, nearby heading, placeholder, name attribute and type
  • Dropdown and radio-button option texts shown on the page
  • The fixed list of generic profile key names (e.g. personal.dob) and their descriptions
  • The names (not values) of any custom fields you created
  • Any of your profile values (name, date of birth, address, numbers…)
  • Your documents or their names
  • Your PIN
  • The page address (URL) or anything you typed into the form
  • Password, card, CVV, bank, OTP and captcha fields (skipped entirely)

Gemini replies with which profile key fits each field. Form Filler then looks up your values and fills them in locally on your computer. The request goes directly from your browser to generativelanguage.googleapis.com using your own API key, so Google's Gemini API terms and privacy policy apply to that request. Note that on Google's free tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products; this content is only field labels and option texts, never your data. Without an API key, Form Filler uses an offline keyword matcher and sends nothing at all.

4. Fields Form Filler will never fill

Passwords, credit/debit card numbers, expiry dates, CVV/CVC, bank account numbers, IFSC/SWIFT/IBAN codes, UPI/ATM PINs, OTPs/verification codes, security questions and captchas. These are detected by field type, autocomplete hints and label text, and are left untouched and not sent to the AI.

5. What Form Filler will never do

  • Never submits a form or clicks a submit button. You always review and submit yourself.
  • Never runs on a page until you click Fill this form on it. Chrome asks your permission for each site the first time; you can remove that permission any time in Settings → Sites with access, or in Chrome's extension settings.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, tracking pixels or remote code. The extension's only network request is the Gemini call described above.
  • No selling or sharing of data. The developer never receives any of your data.

6. Document Locker on web pages

The "Pick from locker" window opens inside a protected extension frame that the web page cannot read. A document is handed to the page only when you click Attach, and only to the upload field you chose. Resizing happens locally.

7. Backups, export and deletion

  • Export backup saves one file that stays encrypted with the PIN in use at the time. Store it safely; anyone with the file and that PIN can read it.
  • Import backup replaces all current data with the backup's contents.
  • Delete all data erases profiles, documents, settings, your PIN data and site permissions from this browser. Uninstalling the extension also removes its stored data.

8. Children

Form Filler does not collect data from anyone. Profiles you create for family members stay on your computer like everything else.

9. Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be listed here with a new date. Questions: open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
